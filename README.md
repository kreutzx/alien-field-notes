## The line

> ★★★★☆ — Lovely planet. The humans queue 40 minutes to photograph a sunset they could watch for free, then rate the photo instead.

## Why this line

Written as a travel review an alien might actually file: a four-star rating up front, genuine affection ("Lovely planet"), then one concrete observed scene — humans queueing 40 minutes to photograph a sunset that was free to watch — with the twist landing at the end: they rate the photo instead of the sunset. One character, one scene, one reversal.
